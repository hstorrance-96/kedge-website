Usage
- Keep the core mark flat; no glow, gradients, or shadows in official logo files.
- Use promotional effects only in marketing artwork.
- Keep generous clear space around the symbol.
- For tiny sizes, use the favicon/mark-only variant.
- The wordmark SVG uses a system sans font as a working draft; convert text to outlines
  and refine letterforms in a vector editor before final commercial production.

Note: These are first-pass, editable concept assets—not a final trademark-cleared
or professionally kerned production identity. The mark geometry is an approximation
of the approved visual direction and can be refined further.
