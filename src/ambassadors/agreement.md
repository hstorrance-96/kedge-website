## 1. Who this is between

This agreement is between **Kedge Fit LLC**, a Michigan limited liability company ("Kedge"), and the person who accepts it on kedge.fit ("you"). Kedge makes Kedge, an iPhone and Apple Watch strength coach.

As an ambassador you share Kedge with people you know, train or reach online. When someone joins with your code or link and becomes a paying member, you earn the amounts in section 3. You must be 18 or older and live in the United States.

This agreement starts when you accept it and Kedge activates your ambassador account.

## 2. Your code and link

- Kedge gives you a personal code and a share link, plus a private ambassador page showing your sign-ups, paying members and earnings.
- Someone who joins with your code or link gets their first month of Kedge Coach Pro free. After that they pay Kedge's normal price through Apple, and can cancel anytime in their Apple settings.
- A member counts for you only if they're new to Kedge and use your code or link when they join. Each person can use one friend or ambassador code, once. If two codes are used, the first one counts.
- You can't use your own code, or create accounts to earn on yourself.
- While you're an active ambassador, Kedge gives you Coach Pro at no cost, with the same coach chat and AI limits as a free month. It ends when this agreement ends; you can then subscribe if you like.

## 3. What you earn and when

| Member's plan | You earn | When it's earned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $15 | When the member completes 2 paid months |
| Monthly | $15 more | When the member completes 6 paid months |
| Monthly | $15 more | When the member completes 12 paid months |
| Yearly | $45 | 120 days after the member's yearly payment is charged, once it's past Apple's refund window |

- **Most you can earn per member:** $45. The free month doesn't count as a paid month.
- **Paid months** are months the member actually paid for and Apple didn't refund. They don't have to be back to back.
- **Switching plans:** if a monthly member switches to yearly, you keep what you've already earned and the yearly $45 is reduced by it, so the total stays $45.
- Nothing is earned on renewals after the first year or on members who never pay.

## 4. Payouts, tax forms and refunds

- **When:** Kedge pays once a month, by the 15th, for everything you earned the month before. Payouts start after Kedge launches on the App Store.
- **How:** direct deposit (ACH) or PayPal, your choice.
- **Before your first payout** you give Kedge a completed IRS Form W-9 and your payout details. Earnings wait, and are paid in full, once Kedge has them. You're responsible for your own taxes; Kedge sends a Form 1099 when the law requires it.
- **Refunds:** if Apple refunds a payment you already earned on, that amount comes off your next payout. If you have no further earnings, Kedge may ask you to pay it back.
- **Minimum:** earnings under $25 carry over to the next month.

## 5. Ground rules

- **Say you earn from it, every time.** Any post, video, message or conversation that shares your code or link must make clear you earn if people join, for example "#ad" or "I get paid if you join Kedge." This is required by the FTC.
- **No medical, injury or weight-loss promises.** Don't say Kedge treats, prevents or cures anything, or promise specific results. Kedge is a coach, not a doctor.
- **Be accurate.** Describe Kedge, its price and the free month as they are.
- **No spam,** no buying followers or sign-ups, no fake or paid App Store reviews, and no posting your code on coupon or deal sites.
- **No paid ads on searches** for "Kedge" or close misspellings, and no ads, accounts or websites that look like they're run by Kedge.
- **Follow each platform's rules** (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and the rest) and the law.

Sign-ups that come from breaking these rules don't earn, and Kedge may end this agreement over them.

## 6. Kedge's name and your content

- You may use the Kedge name, logo and screenshots of the app only to share Kedge under this agreement, and only as Kedge provides them.
- Your posts are yours. You let Kedge repost or share what you publish about Kedge, with credit to you, and you can ask Kedge to take any of it down.
- You keep private anything non-public you learn about Kedge, such as plans, numbers or unreleased features.

## 7. Relationship, changes and ending

- **Independent.** You're not an employee, partner or agent of Kedge, can't sign anything for it, and don't get a salary or benefits.
- **Changes.** Kedge may change the earning amounts or rules with 30 days' notice by email. Changes never reduce what you've already earned.
- **Ending.** Either side can end this agreement anytime by email. Kedge pays what you earned before the end date on the normal schedule, including later milestones for members who joined before then (for example their 6th and 12th paid months), unless it ended because you broke section 5.
- **Whole agreement.** This is the full agreement about the ambassador program. Michigan law applies.
