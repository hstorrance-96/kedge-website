# About Kedge

Kedge is built by one lifter in Michigan, not a big fitness company.

## Who's behind it

I'm Hunter Torrance. I lift, and I built Kedge because I wanted a coach that changes the plan when the day changes, and tells me why.

## Where the coaching comes from

The coach in the app is software. The judgment behind it is human.

- I write the coaching rules myself and review the coach's answers every morning.
- Certified personal trainers are among our beta testers, and their feedback shapes how Kedge coaches.
- When a form video doesn't show enough, the coach says so instead of guessing. If something hurts, it changes the plan instead of pushing you through it.
