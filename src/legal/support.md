# Support

**Contact us:** [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We answer within 1–2 business days.

**Medical emergency?** Stop exercising and call 911 or your local emergency number. The app can't help in an emergency.

## Subscriptions and billing

**How much does it cost?** $29.99 a month or $199.99 a year (US prices), after a 7-day free trial. One plan, everything included.

**How do I cancel?** Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › {{APP}} › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel the subscription.

**Can I get a refund?** Apple handles all refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Can I pause instead?** Yes: Account › Pause my plan. Your plan, history and notes are saved for when you come back.

**I bought on another device and don't see it.** Open the paywall or Account and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

**Why did my chat pause?** Each plan includes a monthly chat allowance, shown as a meter in Account. Workouts, set changes and meal logging keep working when it runs out, and chat resets when your plan renews. Safety questions are always answered.

## Your coach

**Is this medical advice?** No. The coach gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. Check with your doctor before starting, especially if you have a health condition, an injury, or are pregnant.

**Something hurts during a workout.** Stop the set and tap Painful on the set screen. The coach will change today's plan and suggest rehab work. For sharp pain, chest pain, dizziness or fainting, stop and get medical help.

**The coach got something wrong.** Tell it in chat, or long-press the reply and choose Report. Reports go straight to us and help improve the coaching.

**How do form reviews work?** Film a set from the side, about 6–10 feet away, with your whole body in frame. The coach reviews key frames and marks what to fix. Your plan includes 60 a month, and you can buy 20 more anytime.

## Data and privacy

**Who sees my data?** Your coach runs on Claude, made by Anthropic. With your permission, the app sends Anthropic what it needs to coach you, and Anthropic doesn't train its models on it. We never sell your data. Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](#privacy) and [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](#health-data).

**How do I turn off AI sharing?** Account › AI coaching data. The coach pauses until you turn it back on.

**How do I export my data?** Account › Export my data. You get a .zip with spreadsheets that open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, a complete copy in JSON, and your form-review stills. One-tap export comes with a subscription; anyone can email info@kedge.fit for a free copy.

**How do I delete my account?** Account › Delete account. This deletes your data within 30 days. It's free and doesn't require contacting us. Remember to cancel your subscription in Apple settings too.

## Devices

**Which devices work?** iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple Health. Oura and WHOOP are coming. Runs and rides you record in apps like Strava or Garmin Connect come in through Apple Health when that app saves workouts to Health.

**My wearable isn't syncing.** Open Account › Devices, turn the connection off and on again, and make sure the device's own app has synced recently.
