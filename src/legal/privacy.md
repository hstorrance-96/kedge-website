# Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

[COMPANY NAME, LLC] ("we", "us") makes the {{APP}} app, an AI fitness and nutrition coach for adults. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- We collect what you give the app (workouts, meals, check-ins, chat, frames from sets you film) and what you choose to connect (Apple Health, Oura, WHOOP).
- We use it to coach you. That's it.
- Your coach runs on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. With your permission, we send Anthropic what the coach needs to answer you. Anthropic does not train its models on it.
- We never sell your data, never use your health data for advertising, and never share Apple Health data with advertisers or data brokers.
- You can turn off AI sharing, export your data, or delete your account at any time in the app.

## What we collect

**You give us:**

- Account details: name, email (or an Apple private relay email if you use Sign in with Apple).
- Profile and goals: age, height, weight, experience, goals, equipment, schedule, food habits and dislikes.
- Health screening answers: pregnancy status, health conditions, injuries and anything you tell the coach about pain or soreness.
- Training data: workouts, sets, weights, reps, effort ratings, check-ins and energy scores.
- Nutrition data: meals, food photos, barcodes you scan, saved foods.
- Chat and voice: messages you type or speak to the coach. Voice is turned into text to answer you; we don't keep audio recordings after that.
- Still frames from sets you film for form review. Videos you film for form review stay on your phone unless you choose to post one; the app sends only still frames and body-position data for review, and we delete the frames after 90 days unless you save the review.
- Posts, comments and follows, if you use them: what you choose to post (a win from a workout, such as a record, form score, streak or badge, plus a caption, a photo or video you upload, and the stats shown on it), comments and cheers you leave, who you follow and who follows you, and reports and blocks you make.

**From services you connect (only if you turn them on):**

- Apple Health: workouts, steps, heart rate, sleep, body weight and similar data you allow.
- Oura and WHOOP: sleep, recovery, readiness and heart-rate data.
- Apple Health, if you connect it: sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate, wrist temperature, weight, body fat, VO2 max, workouts from other apps, and (only if you turn it on) meals other apps logged. Kedge writes one thing back: your finished Kedge workouts, which you can turn off in Connected devices.
- Apple Watch: heart rate and motion during workouts.

**Automatically:**

- Device type, app version and crash reports, so we can fix bugs.
- Subscription status from Apple. We never see your payment card.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## How we use it

- To build and adjust your training and nutrition plan, answer your questions and review your form.
- To keep you safe, for example by avoiding exercises that conflict with an injury or condition you've told us about.
- To run the app: accounts, subscriptions, reminders you've allowed, support and security.
- To improve the coaching: we look at group statistics, such as how often people finish planned workouts or how hard sets feel, only for groups of 20 or more people and with no names, contact details, messages or individual records. Coach replies you report to us are reviewed to fix mistakes.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it.

## Sharing with Anthropic (third-party AI)

The coach is powered by Claude, from Anthropic, PBC. Before the coach works, the app asks your explicit permission to send Anthropic the data it needs: your messages, workouts, meals and food photos, check-ins, health answers, connected wearable data, and frames from videos you film. Anthropic processes it only to produce the coach's replies, under terms that prohibit training its models on it. You can turn this off in Account at any time; the coach pauses until you turn it back on.

## Posts, comments and followers

**Anything you post is public to the people who can see it. They can view it and could save or share it, so don't post anything you want kept private.** Nothing goes on your profile unless you post it. A post shows the workout or record you picked, plus any caption, photo or video you add (including a set you filmed for form review, if you choose to post it; that uploads the video); it never includes your weight, food, sleep or health details. By default you approve each follower, and only approved followers see your posts and comments on them; if you turn approval off, anyone on Kedge can follow you and see your posts. Comments are visible to the people who can see that post. We count views, cheers and comments on each post to show you how it's doing and to mark posts that are trending. If you share a video outside Kedge, it ends with a short Kedge card showing your name and invite code.

To keep the community safe, every comment, caption, photo and video is checked automatically before anyone else sees it, using Claude from Anthropic under the same no-training terms (for a video, we send a few still frames). Anything that breaks our community rules is hidden, and its poster can lose the ability to comment, add captions or post photos and videos. Reported posts, comments and videos, and anything hidden automatically, may be reviewed by us. Deleting a post deletes its caption, video and comments; deleting your account deletes all of your posts, comments and follows.

## Other service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app, under contracts that limit them to providing that service: cloud hosting and database (Supabase), subscription management (RevenueCat, which receives your purchase records from Apple), crash reporting (Sentry, which receives technical details about crashes but no account, workout, food, health or chat data), email for support ([PROVIDER]), and the USDA FoodData Central database for food lookups (we send only the barcode or food name). On our website, the waitlist form is handled by Formspree, and Brevo stores your email address to send your confirmation and launch emails; we use it only to tell you about Kedge's launch and your waitlist offer, and you can unsubscribe from any email or ask us to delete it. Umami (a cookie-free analytics service) counts visits and which page version you saw; it doesn't identify you or follow you to other sites. If you apply to our beta or ambassador program, Formspree passes us what you enter (such as your name, email, social media links and answers), and we use it only to review your application and run that program. Ambassadors' payout and tax details are handled directly with us and are never stored on our website or in the app.

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data within [30] days, except what we must keep for legal, tax or fraud reasons. One thing we keep for fraud reasons: if you used a friend's invite code or paid for a subscription, we keep a scrambled, one-way code made from your Sign in with Apple ID, with the date. It has no name, email or other data attached and can't be turned back into your Apple ID. It exists only so the same person can't delete their account and claim another free month. Backups clear within a further [35] days. Form-review frames are deleted after 90 days unless you save the review.
