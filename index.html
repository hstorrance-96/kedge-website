Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Kedge Fit LLC ("we", "us") makes the Kedge app, an AI fitness and nutrition coach for adults. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We collect what you give the app (workouts, meals, check-ins, chat, frames from sets you film) and what you choose to connect (Apple Health, Oura, WHOOP).
  • We use it to coach you. That's it.
  • Your coach runs on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. With your permission, we send Anthropic what the coach needs to answer you. Anthropic does not train its models on it.
  • We never sell your data, never use your health data for advertising, and never share Apple Health data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • You can turn off AI sharing, export your data, or delete your account at any time in the app.

What we collect

You give us:

  • Account details: name, email (or an Apple private relay email if you use Sign in with Apple).
  • Profile and goals: age, height, weight, experience, goals, equipment, schedule, food habits and dislikes.
  • Health screening answers: pregnancy status, health conditions, injuries and anything you tell the coach about pain or soreness.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, weights, reps, effort ratings, check-ins and energy scores.
  • Nutrition data: meals, food photos, barcodes you scan, saved foods.
  • Chat and voice: messages you type or speak to the coach. Voice is turned into text to answer you; we don't keep audio recordings after that.
  • Still frames from sets you film for form review. Your videos stay on your phone; the app sends only still frames and body-position data for review, and we delete the frames after 90 days unless you save the review.

From services you connect (only if you turn them on):

  • Apple Health: workouts, steps, heart rate, sleep, body weight and similar data you allow.
  • Oura and WHOOP: sleep, recovery, readiness and heart-rate data.
  • Apple Health, if you connect it: sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate, wrist temperature, weight, body fat, VO2 max, workouts from other apps, and (only if you turn it on) meals other apps logged. Kedge writes one thing back: your finished Kedge workouts, which you can turn off in Connected devices.
  • Apple Watch: heart rate and motion during workouts.

Automatically:

  • Device type, app version, crash reports and basic usage (which screens are used), so we can fix bugs and improve the app.
  • Subscription status from Apple. We never see your payment card.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To build and adjust your training and nutrition plan, answer your questions and review your form.
  • To keep you safe, for example by avoiding exercises that conflict with an injury or condition you've told us about.
  • To run the app: accounts, subscriptions, reminders you've allowed, support and security.
  • To improve the coaching: we review a small sample of coach replies, with names and contact details removed, to check quality. You can opt out in Account.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it.

Sharing with Anthropic (third-party AI)

The coach is powered by Claude, from Anthropic, PBC. Before the coach works, the app asks your explicit permission to send Anthropic the data it needs: your messages, workouts, meals and food photos, check-ins, health answers, connected wearable data, and frames from videos you film. Anthropic processes it only to produce the coach's replies, under terms that prohibit training its models on it. You can turn this off in Account at any time; the coach pauses until you turn it back on.

Other service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app, under contracts that limit them to providing that service: cloud hosting and database (Supabase), subscription management (RevenueCat, which receives your purchase records from Apple), crash reporting (Sentry, which receives technical details about crashes but no account, workout, food, health or chat data), email for support (Brevo), and the USDA FoodData Central database for food lookups (we send only the barcode or food name). On our website, the waitlist form is handled by Formspree, and Brevo stores your email address to send your confirmation and launch emails; we use it only to tell you about Kedge's launch and your waitlist offer, and you can unsubscribe from any email or ask us to delete it. Umami (a cookie-free analytics service) counts visits and which page version you saw; it doesn't identify you or follow you to other sites.

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

Apple Health (HealthKit) data

Data we read from Apple Health is used only to coach you. We never use it for advertising or marketing, never sell it, never share it with data brokers, and never store it in iCloud. It's shared with Anthropic only as described above and only with your permission.

Consumer health data

Some US states, including Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, give extra rights over health data. We collect consumer health data only to provide the coaching you ask for, with your consent, and share it only with Anthropic and our service providers as described above. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for details and how to use your rights.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except what we must keep for legal, tax or fraud reasons. Backups clear within a further 35 days. Form-review frames are deleted after 90 days unless you save the review.

Your choices and rights

  • In the app: edit your profile, disconnect any service, turn off AI sharing, turn off notifications, export your data (paid subscribers; it comes as a .zip with spreadsheets and a complete JSON copy), and delete your account (everyone, free). Anyone, subscribed or not, can email us for a free copy of their data, including anything we keep only on our servers.
  • By request: depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Washington, the EU or UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to opt out of certain processing. Email info@kedge.fit and we'll respond within the time the law requires (usually 30–45 days). We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, keep health data separate and encrypted, limit who can access it, and keep the AI service key on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Age limit

Kedge is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy is for people in Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and other states with consumer health data laws, including Washington's My Health My Data Act. It applies to everyone who uses Kedge, wherever they live, and supplements our main Privacy Policy. If the two conflict on consumer health data, this policy wins.

"Consumer health data" means personal information that identifies, or could reasonably identify, you and relates to your past, present or future physical or mental health.

Consumer health data we collect

  • Health screening answers: age, pregnancy or recent birth, and conditions you tell us about (for example heart conditions, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, joint or back problems, recent surgery or injury, medication for a chronic condition, or an eating disorder).
  • Injuries and symptoms: pain, soreness and injuries you report in check-ins, the pain check or chat.
  • Body measurements: height, weight and body-weight trends.
  • Biometric and wearable data you connect: heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, recovery and readiness scores, and activity from Apple Health, Apple Watch, Oura or WHOOP, plus breathing rate, wrist temperature, body fat and VO2 max from Apple Health. We use these to suggest lighter days and to set calorie and protein targets; they are never sold or used for ads.
  • Exercise and nutrition data: workouts, effort ratings, energy check-ins, meals, food photos and calorie and macro estimates, to the extent they reveal information about your health.
  • Form-review media: video frames of you lifting, and body-position (pose) data extracted from them on your phone.

We don't collect precise location, genetic data or biometric identifiers used to recognize you (such as face geometry), and we don't use geofences around health care facilities.

Where it comes from

  • Directly from you, in the app.
  • From services you choose to connect: Apple Health, Apple Watch, Oura and WHOOP.
  • Created by the app from your data, such as readiness scores, form-review findings and the coach's notes about you.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the coaching you ask for:

  • building and adjusting your training and nutrition plan;
  • keeping you safe, such as avoiding exercises that conflict with an injury or condition, and suggesting you see a doctor when something sounds serious;
  • reviewing your form when you film a set;
  • running and securing your account, and providing support you request.

We don't use consumer health data for advertising or marketing, and we don't use it for any purpose you haven't consented to.

Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with your consent, and only with:

  • Anthropic, PBC, which provides the Claude AI model that powers the coach. It processes your data only to generate the coach's replies and doesn't train its models on it.
  • Service providers (processors) that run the app for us under contracts limiting them to that purpose: cloud hosting and database (Supabase), subscription management (RevenueCat) and crash reporting (Sentry).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect someone's life or safety.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We do not sell consumer health data, and we would never do so without your separate, signed written authorization as the law requires.

Your consent

We ask for your consent in the app before collecting consumer health data and before sharing it with Anthropic. You can withdraw consent at any time:

  • turn off Account › AI coaching data to stop sharing with Anthropic;
  • disconnect any wearable in Account › Devices;

Withdrawing consent stops future collection or sharing. Some features, like the AI coach, can't work without it.

You have the right to:

  • Confirm and access: find out whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, get a copy of it, and get a list of all third parties and affiliates we've shared it with (with their contact details).

  • Not be discriminated against for using any of these rights.
